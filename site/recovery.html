Not fully restored

It does not claim compatibility with historical encrypted files or .dvault files. Preserve old files unchanged for future recovery.

Privacy and limits

This website cannot see local files, access a private vault, start commands on a visitor's computer, or automatically load recovered backups. Do not send personal, genomic, identifying, or biosignal data to hosted chat. The recovered private backups are not published here.
